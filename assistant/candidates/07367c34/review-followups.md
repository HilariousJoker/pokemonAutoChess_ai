## 1. POWDER — requested `speedNerf` vs the stat change that actually happens
- **Candidate says:** "Speed lowered is raw × (1 + AP/100) (× critPower on crit), capped at the victim's current speed, then item adjustments and clamping inside addSpeed", and lists as unresolved that the bodies of `applyBigEaterBeltStatBuff` / `applyTwistBandBuff` "were not located by a search of the pinned tree".
- **Finding:** the helpers exist (see *Shared helpers*, lines ≈1839–1871), so that unresolved item is wrong as stated. The value computed in the strategy is a **request**; the stat change depends on the holder's items, and the later positive restoration is altered too.
- **Source pointers:** `app/core/abilities/powder.ts:54–63` computes `speedNerf = max(victim.speed)(factor × (1 + AP/100) × crit)`, calls `addSpeed(-speedNerf, caster, 0, false)`, and queues a 5000 ms `DelayedCommand` that calls `addSpeed(+speedNerf, caster, 0, false)`. In `addSpeed`: an enemy Powder debuff is **not** scaled by Big Eater Belt (negative value from another team), but a **Twist Band** holder has it flipped into a speed **gain**; the restoration (a positive value) is scaled ×1.25 (rounded down) for a **Big Eater Belt** holder, and the result is clamped to 0…`MAX_SPEED` (300). So the restoration can differ from the nerf, and a Twist Band holder gains speed twice.
- **To resolve (not done):** restate the effect as requested change vs applied change per holder item, remove the "not located" item, keep the remaining unresolved items (Scatterbug / Spewpa passives, Vivillon forms, targettable filter, scan order, 5000 ms timing).

## 2. JUDGEMENT and MULTI_ATTACK — zero raw does not mean zero resolved damage

- **To resolve (not done):** trace a raw 0 from `process` through `handleSpecialDamage` and `handleDamage` for both abilities (including AP and crit factors on 0, shields, and the per-target loop in MULTI_ATTACK), then replace the "deals 0" wording by the traced result.

## 7. HATCH — the generic evolution retry does not establish an immediate second hatch
*Wording corrected in run `run-20261023T200053Z`: the first version of this entry had the finding reversed. The candidate is **not repaired** and stays pending.*
- **Candidate says:** stacks reset to 0 on hatch; unresolved: "Whether stacks is carried across transformPokemon on hatch (transformPokemon was not read)".
- **Finding (corrected):** after a hatch the generic evolution retry does **not** establish an immediate second hatch. The **old** instance's stacks are reset, and `carryOverPermanentStats` does **not** copy stacks to the new instance.
- **Source pointers:** `HatchEvolutionHandler.evolve` (`app/core/evolution-logic/hatch-evolution-handler.ts:18–31`) sets `pokemon.stacks = 0` on the old instance ("prevent trying to evolve twice in a row") and then calls `player.transformPokemon(...)`. `Player.transformPokemon` (`app/models/colyseus-models/player.ts:333–352`) builds the new unit with `PokemonFactory.createPokemonFromName`, calls `carryOverPermanentStats(newPokemon, [pokemon])` and copies dishes, position and items; it does not set `stacks`. `carryOverPermanentStats` (`app/core/evolution-logic/evolution-handler.ts:45–93`) carries over only the permanent deltas of maxHP, atk, def, speDef, speed, ap and luck, and the TM — not `stacks`. The new unit therefore starts at the field default `stacks = 0` (`app/models/colyseus-models/pokemon.ts:103`). The generic retry is `EvolutionManager.afterEvolve` ending with `tryEvolve(pokemonEvolved, player)` (`app/core/evolution-logic/evolution-manager.ts:105–106`); for a HATCH unit `canEvolve` is `stacks >= stacksRequired` (`hatch-evolution-handler.ts:11–16`, `EVOLVE_HATCH = 5`), i.e. `0 >= 5`, false. A chained HATCH unit (the candidate's own evidence lists Dreepy and Drakloak, which both carry a HATCH rule) hatches again only after further stage-end increments (`updateHatch`, `evolution-manager.ts:135–154`; call site `game-commands.ts:1963–1966`).
- **Limits of this reading:** read from the code, not executed; the 2 s `setTimeout` window between the hop and `tryEvolve`, the stage-end loop over the board, and any other path that sets `stacks` on the evolved unit were not traced.
- **To resolve (not done):** reword the candidate's reset behaviour ("stacks resets to 0 on hatch" concerns the old instance; the new instance starts from the factory default) and close its `transformPokemon` unresolved item with the lines above; trace the 2 s window and the stage-end loop.

## 13. CRUNCH — a returned death can concern a Cover Band ally; extend the pilot coverage
*Added in run `run-20261026T044811Z`; the candidate is not repaired.*
- **Candidate says:** "if that hit reports death, the caster heals ceil(50% of the victim's max HP)"; the heal's `condition` is "only when handleSpecialDamage reports death for that hit"; the GUZZLORD passive "can trigger from the kill". Its unresolved items mention only death-cancelling effects such as Shiny Charm and resurrection. (The reviewed pilot record `abilities.CRUNCH` says "if that hit kills it".)
- **Finding:** the `death` flag handed back to the strategy is not necessarily the original target's death. In `PokemonState.handleDamage`, a lethal hit on a unit without a Cover Band is redirected to an adjacent same-team ally holding one (HP above 0), and the call **returns the cover ally's result**. So `{ death: true }` can mean the Cover Band ally died while the original target survived (it keeps the shield loss already applied but loses no HP). Crunch then heals `ceil(0.5 × target.maxHP)` using the **original, surviving** target's max HP, and kill-triggered effects (the GUZZLORD `OnKillEffect` through `triggerDeath` → `attacker.onKill`) belong to the cover ally's death.
- **Source pointers:** `app/core/abilities/crunch.ts:15–25` (the `{ death }` destructuring and `pokemon.handleHeal(Math.ceil(0.5 * target.maxHP), pokemon, 0, false)`); `app/core/pokemon-state.ts:698–724` (Cover Band check, `return coverAlly.handleDamage({ damage: incomingDamage, … })`); `app/core/pokemon-entity.ts:435–465` (`handleSpecialDamage` returns the `handleDamage` result). The item behaviour is already traced in `knowledge/07367c34/item-effects.md` (`### COVER_BAND`, effect `cb-redirect`).
