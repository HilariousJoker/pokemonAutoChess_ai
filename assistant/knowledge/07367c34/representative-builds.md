# Representative builds — reasoned item choices for three unit groups

**Production-branch reference `07367c341fe928763da2b565c2eee010433e4fc1`; deployment unverified.** Units: Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard, the Totodile line (Crunch), Vespiquen (reviewed row modes). Items: the 55 craftable outputs plus Eviolite ([item-effects.md](item-effects.md), [core-mechanics.md](core-mechanics.md) §G). This is **conditional advice derived from reviewed mechanics**: not a tier list, not a claim of an absolute best build, not measured DPS, win rate or meta. Item effects are source-traced only; nothing here was observed in a running game. The numbers are **hand arithmetic under the stated assumptions, not simulated fights and not parity with the combat engine**; `node assistant/check-build-arithmetic.mjs` re-computes them under those same assumptions (a discrete PP-rule model plus closed forms) and checks that this note quotes them.

## Ground rules used
- **Bare vs acquired.** Catalog stats are bare ([lookup](../../lookup-production.mjs)): Charmander/Charmeleon/Charizard HP 60/120/220, ATK 4/8/18, range 1, maxPP 100; Totodile/Croconaw/Feraligatr HP 75/130/240, ATK 7/14/27, range 1, maxPP 100; Vespiquen HP 190, ATK 16, DEF/SPE_DEF 8, maxPP 90. Item bonuses are added to the **fight entity** at fight start (core-mechanics §I) and are not permanent. On evolution, held items move to the evolved unit (3-slot limit applies) and permanent stat deltas carry over ([pilot-evolution.md](pilot-evolution.md)), so a build chosen on a 1★ persists through evolution; raw ability numbers and bare stats grow with stars.
- **Counting model (W1–W7).** PP rule only: each basic attack gives +5 PP; a unit casts instead of attacking when `pp ≥ maxPP`, then `pp −= maxPP`; **no PP from damage taken** (it would shorten every count). Results are **counts of basic attacks, PP requirements, periodic ticks and damage per cast**. They are deliberately not times: a real fight starts entities in MovingState (movement before contact), schedules each basic attack through a delayed attack command, and a periodic effect's timer is reset when it fires instead of catching up missed intervals (so tick counts depend on how long the unit is actually alive and acting, and are not computed here). Do not read any count as a cast time or as an item ranking.
- **Soul Dew:** each *successful* tick gives +5 AP and +5 PP, i.e. exactly one basic attack's worth of PP (for a 100-PP caster, 20 ticks = 100 PP and 100 AP).
- **Damage:** Blast Burn / Crunch / order bonuses are raw × (1 + AP/100) before the target's defense; ability casts crit only with `ABILITY_CRIT` (or by-default crit), and neither Blast Burn nor Crunch crits by default.

## 1. Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard (Blast Burn)
**Ability and role.** Blast Burn is special damage (raw **30 / 60 / 120** by stars) to **every enemy in the 8 cells around the caster**, not around its target; allies are untouched ([pilot-abilities.md](pilot-abilities.md)). Range is 1 and the cast needs a target in range, so this is a melee front-row caster whose value scales with how many enemies stand next to it. It can only cast if it survives long enough to fill 100 PP.

| Goal | Useful covered items | What they do (numbers from the records) |
|---|---|---|
| Damage per cast | Choice Specs (+100 AP), Heavy-Duty Boots (+50 AP, +12 DEF), Pokemonomicon (+30 AP; burn + −1 SPE_DEF on each target hit), Dawn Stone (+20 AP), Soul Dew (+5 AP per tick), Reaper Cloth (+10 AP; lets casts crit at 30 %) | Flat AP multiplies raw damage; Pokemonomicon adds a percentage-of-max-HP burn to every target the cast hits |
| Casting frequency | Soul Dew (+5 PP per tick), Deep Sea Tooth (+15 PP start, +5 PP per attack, +15 on a kill, +7 ATK), Aqua Egg (+30 PP, PP back after each cast), Water Stone (+30 PP), Efficient Bandanna (max PP ×0.85, +15 PP for the holder) | Fewer basic attacks per 100 PP |
| Survival | King's Rock (+100 HP, shield 20 % of max HP), Rocky Helmet (+25 DEF, cancels crit bonus), Cover Band, Assault Vest (+40 SPE_DEF), Poké Doll (×0.7), Shell Bell (heals from Blast Burn damage per target) | See W3, W5 |

**W1 — immediate AP vs periodic AP/PP (1★, raw 30).** Choice Specs: every cast deals raw 60, with no change to how many attacks a cast needs (20 with PP from attacks only). Soul Dew: after k successful ticks the cast deals raw 30 × (1 + 5k/100) (k = 10 → 45, 20 → 60, 40 → 90), and each of those ticks replaces one basic attack, so the first cast needs 20 − k attacks (k = 5 → 15, k = 10 → 10). The tradeoff: Specs hits harder on every cast until Soul Dew has ticked 20 times; Soul Dew produces casts sooner and its later casts overtake Specs' 60 once k > 20. Which is better depends on how many casts the unit actually gets to make (its survival, how often it is in contact, other PP sources such as Aqua Egg or damage taken) and on how many ticks it receives before each cast. With few or early casts, Specs' immediate AP counts more; with many casts or a weak PP supply, Soul Dew's PP and growing AP count more. Both can be held (two slots).

**W2 — starting PP vs smaller max PP vs PP return (1★, basic attacks between casts, PP from attacks only).** None: 20, 20, 20. Water Stone (+30 PP once): 14, then 20, 20. Efficient Bandanna **holder** (max PP 85, start 15): 14, then 17, 17; a **neighbor** of the holder gets 85 without the +15: 17, 17, 17. Deep Sea Tooth (start 15, +10 PP per attack): 9, then 10, 10. **Aqua Egg** (start 30; returns round(20 + 2n) PP after cast n): 14, 16, 15. So Water Stone helps only the first cast, Efficient Bandanna saves 3 attacks every cast, Aqua Egg saves more as casts accumulate. Deep Sea Tooth needs the unit to be basic-attacking; Soul Dew does not.

**W3 — survival: King's Rock vs Rocky Helmet (HP+shield or DEF only, no other modifiers).** 1★ (HP 60, DEF 3): King's Rock pool 160 HP + 32 shield = 192 (**×3.2** against any damage type); Rocky Helmet DEF 28 cuts physical damage taken to ×0.479 (**×2.09**, physical only, plus no crit bonus against it). 2★: ×2.2 vs ×2.04. 3★ (HP 220, DEF 5): King's Rock 320 + 64 = 384 (×1.75) vs Rocky Helmet ×2.00 physical (×1.0 against special). So King's Rock is the broader choice; Rocky Helmet overtakes only for Charizard facing mostly physical damage or crits.

**W4 — Pokemonomicon vs Choice Specs by enemy HP (burn: 5 % max HP true per tick, about 2–3 ticks in its 3000 ms; tick count read from the burn code, untested).** 1★: Specs adds 30 raw per target; Pokemonomicon adds 9 raw + 10–15 % of the target's max HP, so it is ahead once targets have more than about 140–210 max HP (ignoring SPE_DEF, which only lowers the Specs side). 3★: Specs adds 120, Pokemonomicon 36 + burn: ahead above about 560–840 max HP. Both need the targets to take the hit; burn can be blocked (immunity, Rune Protect).

**W5 — Shell Bell on an AoE.** Blast Burn at AP 0 deals 27 per target (raw 30 vs SPE_DEF 3). Shell Bell heals ceil(33 %) of damage dealt per target: 9 each, **36 with four adjacent enemies** (AP 100: 18 each, 72). A Charmander has 60 bare HP, so this is large at 1★ and scales with enemies in the ring.

**Default (assumptions: front-row melee, enemies reach it, it survives to attack repeatedly, no extra PP sources).** **Soul Dew + Deep Sea Tooth + King's Rock** (Rocky Helmet instead of King's Rock at 3★ against mostly physical teams). Reason: together they cut the attacks needed per cast (Deep Sea Tooth alone 9 for the first cast and 10 afterwards; every Soul Dew tick removes one more) while Soul Dew adds AP, the unit's weak ATK 4 becomes 11, and the third slot buys the HP it needs to stay in the ring. Components: Soul Dew = Twisted Spoon + Mystic Water, Deep Sea Tooth = Mystic Water + Charcoal, King's Rock = Miracle Seed ×2. This is a reasonable default, not a ranking of the alternatives.
**Other good options and when they fit:** Choice Specs (immediate AP; W1) when you expect few casts or already have PP sources; Pokemonomicon vs high-HP clustered targets (W4); Shell Bell when several enemies are adjacent and you need sustain (W5); Aqua Egg or Water Stone for an earlier first cast without basic attacks (W2); Rocky Helmet at 3★ vs physical damage (W3).
**Anti-synergies.** **Nullify Bandanna** (cannot cast). **Wide Lens**: range +2 does not enlarge the 8-cell ring; a distant cast can hit nothing if no enemy is adjacent (inference). **Eviolite** blocks evolving. **Smoke Ball** triggers only if the unit survives a hit below 40 % HP, then flies away from the ring (positioning cost). Efficient Bandanna with Aqua Egg: the egg's return shrinks because it depends on max PP (85: round(17 + 2n)).

## 2. Totodile → Croconaw → Feraligatr (Crunch)
**Ability and role.** Crunch is **single-target** special damage, raw **40 / 80 / 160** by stars; if it kills the target the caster heals ceil(50 % of the victim's max HP) (not AP-scaled). Melee front-row fighter; Croconaw and Feraligatr use the same strategy (declared, outside the reviewed unit set).

**W6 — AP or ATK? (per 100-PP cycle: 20 basic attacks plus one cast; PP from attacks only).** 1★: basic attacks 20 × 7 = **140** raw physical versus Crunch **40**. Choice Specs adds +40 raw per cast; **Red Orb** (+10 ATK) adds 20 × 10 = **+200** per cycle (a quarter of each attack becomes true damage): about five times the gain at 1★. 3★ (ATK 27, Crunch 160): basic 540 vs 160; Specs +160, Red Orb +200: only 1.25× apart, so AP is a real alternative at 3★, especially if casts come more often than once per 20 attacks (e.g. with PP items) or the target's SPE_DEF is lower than its DEF. These are raw amounts before DEF/SPE_DEF and ignore the true-damage share.

**Default (assumptions: front-row melee; basic-attack heavy; it survives to attack repeatedly).** **Deep Sea Tooth + Red Orb + King's Rock** (Rocky Helmet if opponents are mainly physical or crit-heavy). Deep Sea Tooth cuts the first cast to 9 basic attacks and adds +20 PP on a basic-attack kill; King's Rock keeps it alive (1★: ×2.8 vs Rocky Helmet ×2.04).
**Other good options and when they fit:** Punching Glove (+8 % target max HP per basic attack) vs high-HP targets; Soul Dew when kills (and Crunch heals) are common and you want more casts; Choice Specs or Pokemonomicon at 3★ or with frequent casts (Pokemonomicon's burn and −1 SPE_DEF are separate from Crunch's modest AP scaling); Shell Bell for sustain.
**Anti-synergies.** **Nullify Bandanna** (no casts: you lose the kill heal; each basic attack then spends its PP as extra special damage); Eviolite while you want to evolve. Wide Lens is not harmful here (single target), but its positioning effect on a melee brawler was not traced.

## 3. Vespiquen (row modes)
**Role and positioning.** Obtainable only through paths not traced here; stats are bare (HP 190, ATK 16, range by row). **Moving it to a row sets its mode** (manual position change; fixed at fight start): row **1** = ATTACK_ORDER (range 3), row **2** = HEAL_ORDER (range 2), row **3** (front) = DEFEND_ORDER (range 1). A Vespiquen made by evolution and never moved has an untraced mode. A cast spawns an allied Combee only if a free cell exists, and Combees can die, so the Combee count N is **the number alive when the effect is evaluated**, not a guaranteed growth per cast.
- **ATTACK:** next basic attack deals extra special damage (60 + 30N) × (1 + AP/100); Combees are enraged 3 s.
- **HEAL:** the caster and each Combee heal adjacent allies 30 each, scaled by Vespiquen's AP/crit (**newly inspected**: `abilities/heal-order.ts:47` calls `handleHeal(heal, pokemon, 1, crit)` with Vespiquen as caster), and clear negative statuses.
- **DEFEND:** Combees get a 30 shield and Vespiquen 30 + 30N (reviewed); the Combee shields are scaled by Vespiquen's AP and crit (**newly inspected**: `abilities/defend-order.ts:42` calls `addShield(shield, pokemon, 1, crit)`).

**W7 — casting engine vs AP (maxPP 90, PP from attacks only).** Basic attacks between casts: none 18, 18, 18; Aqua Egg (start 30, returns round(18 + 2n)) 12, 14, 14; Efficient Bandanna **holder** (max PP round(90 × 0.85) = 77, start 15, so 62 more PP: ceil(62 / 5) = **13** attacks) then 15, 16; a **neighbor** of the holder (77 without the +15) 16, 15, 16. Each Soul Dew tick replaces one attack (18 − k). ATTACK bonus with N Combees alive: AP 0: N=1 → 90, N=3 → 150, N=5 → 210; AP 100: 180 / 300 / 420. One cycle is 18 basic attacks (16 ATK) = 288 raw physical; Red Orb adds +180 per cycle, Choice Specs adds +90 to +210 per cast depending on N. So AP and ATK are close in ATTACK mode; casting frequency raises how often the bonus fires and, when spawns succeed and Combees survive, N.

**Complete suggested builds (three items each; assumptions: PP from attacks only, the unit survives to cast repeatedly).**

| Mode | Suggestion | Prefer another when |
|---|---|---|
| ATTACK, row 1 | Soul Dew + Aqua Egg + Choice Specs (without a spoon pair: Soul Dew + Aqua Egg + Red Orb) | N is usually large → AP items gain; spawns fail or Combees die → favor ATK |
| HEAL, row 2 | Soul Dew + Choice Specs + King's Rock (AP scales every heal) | Allies rarely take damage → drop Choice Specs for Aqua Egg; you are focused → Rocky Helmet over King's Rock |
| DEFEND, row 3 | Soul Dew + King's Rock + Rocky Helmet (bare DEF 8: King's Rock ×1.83, Rocky Helmet ×1.89 against physical) | Opponents mostly special → Choice Specs or Aqua Egg in place of Rocky Helmet; no Aqua Egg in this build |

**Anti-synergies.** **Nullify Bandanna** (no Combee, no modes); Eviolite cannot be equipped (Vespiquen declares no evolution); Wide Lens adds range on top of the mode's range (interaction untraced; inference only).

## Limits
Source-traced, not gameplay-tested; counts are PP-rule arithmetic, not cast times: movement, delayed attack commands, periodic-timer behavior, damage-taken PP, statuses, targeting, ring occupancy and real fight length are not modelled; synergy effects of the units' types are not audited; acquisition of Vespiquen and the Eevee/Type Null style evolution handlers are untraced.
