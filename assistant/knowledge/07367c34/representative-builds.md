# Representative builds — reasoned item choices for three unit groups

**Production-branch reference `07367c341fe928763da2b565c2eee010433e4fc1`; deployment unverified.** Units: Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard, the Totodile line (Crunch), Vespiquen (reviewed row modes). Items: the 55 craftable outputs plus Eviolite ([item-effects.md](item-effects.md), [core-mechanics.md](core-mechanics.md) §G). This is **conditional advice derived from reviewed mechanics**: not a tier list, not a claim of an absolute best build, not measured DPS, win rate or meta. Item effects are source-traced only; nothing here was observed in a running game. Numbers are hand arithmetic from formulas whose applicability is established; they are **not simulated battles**. They were re-computed independently by `node assistant/check-build-arithmetic.mjs` (a slot-by-slot timeline versus closed forms).

## Ground rules used
- **Bare vs acquired.** Catalog stats are bare ([lookup](../../lookup-production.mjs)): Charmander/Charmeleon/Charizard HP 60/120/220, ATK 4/8/18, speed 57, range 1, maxPP 100; Totodile/Croconaw/Feraligatr HP 75/130/240, ATK 7/14/27, speed 50, range 1, maxPP 100; Vespiquen HP 190, ATK 16, DEF/SPE_DEF 8, speed 38, maxPP 90. Item bonuses are added to the **fight entity** at fight start (core-mechanics §I) and are not permanent. On evolution, held items move to the evolved unit (3-slot limit applies) and permanent stat deltas carry over ([pilot-evolution.md](pilot-evolution.md)), so a build chosen on a 1★ carries through evolution; the raw ability numbers and bare stats grow with stars.
- **Timeline assumptions (W1–W3, W6, W7):** the unit attacks continuously from fight start (+5 PP per basic attack; a cast uses an attack slot, then PP −= maxPP); nominal attack interval `round(1000 / (0.4 + 0.007 × speed))` = 1252 ms (speed 57), 1333 ms (speed 50), 1502 ms (speed 38), time unit assumed ms; **no PP from damage taken** (it would shorten every time shown); Soul Dew ticks at 1000 ms. Times are quantized to attack slots.
- **Damage:** Blast Burn / Crunch / order bonuses are raw × (1 + AP/100) before the target's defense; ability casts crit only with `ABILITY_CRIT` (or by-default crit), and neither Blast Burn nor Crunch crits by default.

## 1. Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard (Blast Burn)
**Ability and role.** Blast Burn is special damage (raw **30 / 60 / 120** by stars) to **every enemy in the 8 cells around the caster**, not around its target; allies are untouched ([pilot-abilities.md](pilot-abilities.md)). Range is 1 and the cast needs a target in range, so this is a melee front-row caster whose value scales with how many enemies stand next to it. It can only cast if it survives long enough to fill 100 PP.

| Goal | Useful covered items | What they do (numbers from the records) |
|---|---|---|
| Damage per cast | Choice Specs (+100 AP), Heavy-Duty Boots (+50 AP, +12 DEF), Pokemonomicon (+30 AP; burn + −1 SPE_DEF on each target hit), Dawn Stone (+20 AP), Soul Dew (+5 AP/s), Reaper Cloth (+10 AP; lets casts crit at 30 %) | Flat AP multiplies raw damage; Pokemonomicon adds a percentage-of-max-HP burn to every target the cast hits |
| Casting frequency | Soul Dew (+5 PP/s), Deep Sea Tooth (+15 PP start, +5 PP per attack, +15 on a kill, +7 ATK), Aqua Egg (+30 PP, PP back after each cast), Water Stone (+30 PP), Efficient Bandanna (max PP ×0.85, +15 PP for the holder) | Shortens the time to each 100 PP |
| Survival | King's Rock (+100 HP, shield 20 % of max HP), Rocky Helmet (+25 DEF, cancels crit bonus), Cover Band, Assault Vest (+40 SPE_DEF), Poké Doll (×0.7), Shell Bell (heals from Blast Burn damage per target) | See W3, W5 |

**W1 — immediate vs ramping AP (1★, raw 30, PP from attacks only).** Casts happen at: no item 26.3 s, 52.6 s; **Choice Specs** the same times but raw 60 each; **Soul Dew** 12.5 s (AP 60 → raw 48), 23.8 s (AP 115 → 64.5), 35.1 s (AP 175 → 82.5), 47.6 s (AP 235 → 100.5). Raw per target by 47.6 s: Soul Dew 295.5, Choice Specs 60 (one cast; its second is at 52.6 s). Soul Dew's AP only equals Specs' 100 after 20 ticks (20 s), but it also moves every cast earlier. So: **Soul Dew** when casts depend on the unit's own PP and fights last past ~15 s; **Choice Specs** when other PP sources (Aqua Egg, Water Stone, damage taken) bring most casts before ~20 s.

**W2 — starting PP vs reduced max PP vs PP return (1★).** Basic attacks before the first cast: none 20 (26.3 s); Water Stone or Aqua Egg 14 (18.8 s); Efficient Bandanna **holder** 14 (18.8 s), a **neighbor** of the holder 17 (22.5 s); Deep Sea Tooth 9 (12.5 s). Later casts: Water Stone 45.1 s, 71.4 s; Efficient holder 41.3 s, 63.9 s; **Aqua Egg** 40.1 s, 60.1 s, 80.1 s (it returns round(20 + 2n) PP after cast n: 22, 24, 26…, so it pulls ahead of Water Stone after the first cast). Soul Dew + Deep Sea Tooth: 7.5 s, 16.3 s, 25.0 s. Deep Sea Tooth needs the unit to be basic-attacking in contact; Soul Dew does not.

**W3 — survival: King's Rock vs Rocky Helmet (HP+shield or DEF only, no other modifiers).** 1★ (HP 60, DEF 3): King's Rock pool 160 HP + 32 shield = 192 (**×3.2** against any damage type); Rocky Helmet DEF 28 cuts physical damage taken to ×0.479 (**×2.09**, physical only, plus no crit bonus against it). 2★: ×2.2 vs ×2.04. 3★ (HP 220, DEF 5): King's Rock 320 + 64 = 384 (×1.75) vs Rocky Helmet ×2.00 physical (×1.0 against special). So King's Rock is the broader choice; Rocky Helmet overtakes only for Charizard facing mostly physical damage or crits.

**W4 — Pokemonomicon vs Choice Specs by enemy HP (burn: 5 % max HP true per tick, about 2–3 ticks in its 3000 ms; tick count read from the burn code, untested).** 1★: Specs adds 30 raw per target; Pokemonomicon adds 9 raw + 10–15 % of the target's max HP, so it is ahead once targets have more than about 140–210 max HP (ignoring SPE_DEF, which only lowers the Specs side). 3★: Specs adds 120, Pokemonomicon 36 + burn: ahead above about 560–840 max HP. Both need the targets to take the hit; burn can be blocked (immunity, Rune Protect).

**W5 — Shell Bell on an AoE.** Blast Burn at AP 0 deals 27 per target (raw 30 vs SPE_DEF 3). Shell Bell heals ceil(33 %) of damage dealt per target: 9 each, **36 with four adjacent enemies** (AP 100: 18 each, 72). A Charmander has 60 bare HP, so this is large at 1★ and scales with enemies in the ring.

**Default (assumptions: front-row melee, enemies reach it, fights of at least ~15 s of contact, no extra PP sources).** **Soul Dew + Deep Sea Tooth + King's Rock** (Rocky Helmet instead at 3★ against mostly physical teams). Reason: the first cast moves from 26.3 s to about 7.5 s with AP ≈ 35, the unit's weak ATK 4 becomes 11, and the third slot buys the HP it needs to stay in the ring. Components: Soul Dew = Twisted Spoon + Mystic Water, Deep Sea Tooth = Mystic Water + Charcoal, King's Rock = Miracle Seed ×2.
**Prefer something else when:** you already have strong PP sources and short fights → Choice Specs (W1); enemies have high max HP and are clustered → Pokemonomicon (W4); several enemies are adjacent and you need sustain → Shell Bell (W5); you want a first cast early without basic attacks → Aqua Egg/Water Stone (W2).
**Anti-synergies.** **Nullify Bandanna** (cannot cast). **Wide Lens**: range +2 does not enlarge the 8-cell ring; a distant cast can hit nothing if no enemy is adjacent (inference). **Eviolite** blocks evolving. **Smoke Ball** triggers only if it survives a hit below 40 % HP, then flies away from the ring (positioning cost). Efficient Bandanna with Aqua Egg: the egg's return shrinks because it depends on max PP (85: round(17 + 2n)).

## 2. Totodile → Croconaw → Feraligatr (Crunch)
**Ability and role.** Crunch is **single-target** special damage, raw **40 / 80 / 160** by stars; if it kills the target the caster heals ceil(50 % of the victim's max HP) (not AP-scaled). Melee front-row fighter; Croconaw and Feraligatr use the same strategy (declared, outside the reviewed unit set).

**W6 — AP or ATK? (per 100-PP cycle, PP from attacks only).** A cycle is 20 basic attacks plus one cast. 1★: basic attacks 20 × 7 = **140** raw physical versus Crunch **40**. Choice Specs adds +40 raw per cast; **Red Orb** (+10 ATK) adds 20 × 10 = **+200** (a quarter of the attack becomes true damage). 3★ (ATK 27, Crunch 160): basic 540 vs 160; Specs +160, Red Orb +200. So **ATK and PP items beat AP items** for this line at every star level; the kill-heal rewards frequent casts, not AP.

**Default (assumptions: front-row melee; basic-attack heavy; fights ≥ ~15 s).** **Deep Sea Tooth + Red Orb + King's Rock** (Rocky Helmet if opponents are mainly physical or crit-heavy). First cast moves from 28.0 s to 13.3 s; Deep Sea Tooth's +20 PP on a basic-attack kill speeds repeats. Totodile's survival gains are comparable to Charmander's (1★: King's Rock ×2.8 vs Rocky Helmet ×2.04).
**Prefer something else when:** enemies have high max HP → Punching Glove (+8 % target max HP per basic attack); kills are common and you want more Crunch heals → Soul Dew (first cast 13.3 s) in place of Red Orb; you need execution safety → keep a survival item.
**Anti-synergies.** Choice Specs/Dawn Stone/Pokemonomicon as the main plan (AP barely moves Crunch); **Nullify Bandanna** (no casts: you lose the kill heal; each basic attack then spends its PP as extra special damage); Eviolite while you want to evolve. Wide Lens is not harmful here (single target), but its positioning effect on a melee brawler was not traced.

## 3. Vespiquen (row modes)
**Role and positioning.** Obtainable only through paths not traced here; stats are bare (HP 190, ATK 16, range by row). **Moving it to a row sets its mode** (manual position change; fixed at fight start): row **1** = ATTACK_ORDER (range 3), row **2** = HEAL_ORDER (range 2), row **3** (front) = DEFEND_ORDER (range 1). A Vespiquen made by evolution and never moved has an untraced mode. Every cast spawns an allied Combee (if a cell is free), so each cast increases the Combee count N.
- **ATTACK:** next basic attack deals extra special damage (60 + 30N) × (1 + AP/100); Combees are enraged 3 s.
- **HEAL:** the caster and each Combee heal adjacent allies 30 each, scaled by Vespiquen's AP/crit (**newly inspected**: `abilities/heal-order.ts:47` calls `handleHeal(heal, pokemon, 1, crit)` with Vespiquen as caster), and clear negative statuses.
- **DEFEND:** Combees get a 30 shield and Vespiquen 30 + 30N (reviewed); the Combee shields are scaled by Vespiquen's AP and crit (**newly inspected**: `abilities/defend-order.ts:42` calls `addShield(shield, pokemon, 1, crit)`).

**W7 — casting engine vs AP (speed 38, maxPP 90, interval 1502 ms).** First casts: none 28.5 s; **Soul Dew 12.0 s (AP 60)**, 24.0 s (AP 120); Soul Dew + Aqua Egg 9.0 s, 18.0 s, 27.0 s; Efficient Bandanna (maxPP 77) 25.5 s. ATTACK bonus with N Combees: AP 0: N=1 → 90, N=3 → 150, N=5 → 210; AP 100: 180 / 300 / 420. One cycle is 18 basic attacks (16 ATK) = 288 raw physical; Red Orb adds +180 per cycle, Choice Specs adds +90 to +210 per cast depending on N. So AP and ATK are close; **casting frequency also grows N**, so Soul Dew (AP and PP) is the stronger common core.

| Mode | Default suggestion (assumptions: PP from attacks only, fights ≥ ~15 s) | Prefer another when |
|---|---|---|
| ATTACK, row 1 | Soul Dew + Aqua Egg + Choice Specs (or Deep Sea Tooth/Red Orb without a spoon pair) | Many Combee already out (large N) → AP items gain; few free cells → fewer Combee, favor ATK |
| HEAL, row 2 | Soul Dew + Choice Specs (AP scales every heal) + a survival item | Allies rarely take damage → casting engine matters less than shield/survival |
| DEFEND, row 3 | Soul Dew + King's Rock + Rocky Helmet (bare DEF 8: King's Rock ×1.83, Rocky Helmet ×1.89 against physical) | Opponents mostly special → King's Rock over Rocky Helmet |

**Anti-synergies.** **Nullify Bandanna** (no Combee, no modes); Eviolite cannot be equipped (Vespiquen declares no evolution); Wide Lens adds range on top of the mode's range (interaction untraced; inference only).
