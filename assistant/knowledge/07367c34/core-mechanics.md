## A. Damage and defenses
**In short.** Every hit has a *type*. **Physical** damage is reduced by the target's **DEF**, **special** damage by its **SPE_DEF**, and **true** damage skips the defense division (and the flat reductions) but **not** the shared ceil/min-1 step below. The normal reduction is `damage ÷ (1 + 0.05 × defense)`, rounded **up** with a minimum of 1 — a final ceil/min-1 step that, in this code path, also applies to true damage. Shields absorb damage before HP.

- *Declared:* `ARMOR_FACTOR = 0.05` (`app/config/game/battle.ts:5`).
- *Traced — the reduction step* (`app/core/pokemon-state.ts:handleDamage`, `:559–566`): physical → `damage / (1 + ARMOR_FACTOR × def)`; special → `… × speDef`; true → unchanged. Then, for non-true types only, some flat reductions apply (Poké Doll ×0.7, Guts/Sturdy/Defiant/Coaching −3/−6/−9/−12, Wonder Guard −20; `:568–595`). The next line, `reducedDamage = max(1, ceil(reducedDamage))` (`:597`), is **outside** that non-true block, so it applies to **every** attack type in this path, true damage included. Shields take the damage first, the remainder hits HP (`:618–649`, `:726`). A target with *armor reduction* uses half its defenses (`:509–514`).
- *Traced — where the "incoming damage" comes from.* A basic attack starts from the attacker's ATK (`pokemon-state.ts:attack`, `:36`), is physical unless the attacker has the SPECIAL_ATTACKS effect (`:41–43`), is multiplied on a crit by `1 + (critPower − 1) × reduction` (`:214–217`; default crit chance 10 %, power 2 — `app/config/game/battle.ts:8–9`) and rounded (`:235`); ability damage is dealt by the ability code, usually through `handleSpecialDamage`, which first adds the attacker's AP (`damage × (1 + AP/100)`, `app/core/pokemon-entity.ts:399–400`, default `apBoost = true`).
- *Arithmetic example (assumptions: no crit, no AP, no shield/status/item/weather/field modifiers, target hp ≥ the damage):* a physical hit of **20** on a target with **DEF 10**: `20 ÷ (1 + 0.05 × 10) = 13.33…` → ceil → **14** HP. The same 20 as special on SPE_DEF 10 is also 14; as true damage it is 20 (a fractional true hit such as 20.2 is also rounded up, to 21, and anything below 1 becomes 1).
- *Executed* (real `handleDamage`, stub target, no modifiers): 30 physical vs DEF 10 → 20; 30 special vs SPE_DEF 20 → 15; 30 true → 30; 1 physical vs DEF 100 → 1 (the floor); true 30.2 → 31 and true 0.2 → 1 (the ceil/min-1 step also applies to true damage); 30 vs DEF 0 → 30; 30 physical vs DEF 10 with a 10 shield → shield 10 → 0 and HP −10 (reduced hit 20).
- **Not universal / unresolved:** many attacker- and target-side multipliers sit *before* the defense step (enrage ×2, Electric/Psychic/Grass/Fairy fields ×1.2, Misty/Bloodmoon weather ×1.2, Sheer Cold ×1.3, …; `pokemon-state.ts:455–507`), and others around it (magic bounce, protect, dodge, Power Lens, Shiny Charm, Fossil, resurrection; death rules `:650–800`). They were **not** traced one by one; the formula above is the *defense step only*.

## B. PP (mana) and casting
**In short.** Each unit has a PP bar with maximum `maxPP`. A unit **casts instead of basic-attacking** when its PP reaches `maxPP`. PP comes mainly from **attacking** (+5 per basic attack) and from **taking damage** (+⌈damage that got past the shield ÷ 10⌉; for an ordinary non-lethal hit this equals the HP lost, but it is not defined as "HP lost" — see below). After a cast the bar is reduced by `maxPP`, so extra PP carries over.

- *Declared:* `ON_ATTACK_MANA = 5` (`battle.ts:3`).
- *Traced — gain:* `onAttack` adds 5 (`pokemon-entity.ts:911–932`); `handleDamage` adds `ceil(residualDamage / 10)` to the target when the hit is flagged to give mana (`pokemon-state.ts:730–732`). `residualDamage` is the reduced damage left after the shield; HP is clamped separately (`hp = max(0, hp − residualDamage)`, `:726`), so on an **overkill** hit the PP gain is based on the full residual damage, not on the smaller HP actually lost. In an ordinary non-lethal hit the two are the same number. `addPP` clamps the bar to `0 … 2×maxPP − 1`, blocks positive gains while silenced, protected, resurrecting or under NO_PP_GAIN, and halves positive gains under fatigue (`pokemon-entity.ts:508–532`). PP starts at 0 in a fight (field default `pokemon-entity.ts:97`).
- *Traced — trigger:* each time the attack cooldown ends, the unit casts if `pp >= maxPP && canCast` (`app/core/attacking-state.ts:92–94`), otherwise it basic-attacks; the cast uses that attack slot. `canCast` is false when silenced, holding Nullify Bandana, or with a pending Teleport (`pokemon-entity.ts:250–256`). Abilities flagged `requiresTarget = false` may cast without a target (`app/core/moving-state.ts:40–45`).
- *Traced — after casting:* `AbilityStrategy.process` sets `pp = max(0, pp − maxPP)` and counts the cast (`app/core/abilities/ability-strategy.ts:16–17`); `castAbility` can roll a crit only with ABILITY_CRIT (or a `canCritByDefault` ability) (`app/core/abilities/cast.ts:18–26`).
- **Exceptions seen, not catalogued:** items/effects that add or remove PP (Shiny Charm +50 PP, Nullify Bandana, Twist Band, …), abilities that manipulate PP themselves, and per-ability rules (see [pilot-abilities.md](pilot-abilities.md) for three examples).

## C. Attack speed
**In short.** The "speed" stat is **not** attacks per second. The wait between a unit's attack decisions is `round(1000 / (0.4 + 0.007 × speed))` in the simulation's time units (milliseconds, see caveat), so higher speed shortens the wait with diminishing returns.

- *Traced:* `AttackingState.update` resets the cooldown with `resetCooldown(1000, speed)` (`attacking-state.ts:22–24`; paralysis halves the speed used); `PokemonEntity.resetCooldown` computes `round(baseDuration / (0.4 + speed × 0.007))` (`pokemon-entity.ts:319–321`). `getAttackTimings` uses the same factor for the animation (`attacking-state.ts:126–146`).
- *Executed* (real `resetCooldown` / `getAttackTimings`): speed 0 → 2500; 50 (the default, `battle.ts:7`) → 1333; 100 → 909; 200 → 556; 300 → 400. *Arithmetic:* the reciprocals are ≈ 0.40, 0.75, 1.10, 1.80 and 2.50 — **nominal** attack rates (the cooldown alone, uninterrupted, *if* one cooldown unit is one millisecond), not guaranteed observed attacks per second: movement, targeting, crowd control, casts that use the attack slot and projectile timing were not folded in.
- *Caveat (inferred):* the cooldown is decreased by the simulation delta passed from the room (`game-commands.ts:1178`, from Colyseus' simulation interval); that this delta is in milliseconds was **not** traced into the library, but the `1000`/`500` constants and comments assume it.
- Movement is separate: one cell costs `round(500 / (0.5 + speed/100))` units (`moving-state.ts:23`, `app/core/move-speed.ts:3–9`; executed factor 1 at speed 50). Buffs/debuffs on speed and how range and travel time affect when damage lands (`attacking-state.ts:131–144`) were not traced further.

## D. Positioning
**In short.** On the player board, **row 0 is the bench** and **rows 1–3** are the battlefield; **row 1 is the back row and row 3 the front row** for both teams. Placement only decides where a unit *starts*; during combat units walk toward enemies.

- *Declared/Traced:* units can only be dropped on `y < BOARD_SIDE_HEIGHT` (= 4, "0 = bench"; `app/config/game/board.ts:3`, `game-commands.ts:335–343`). At fight start the simulation copies only non-bench units (`app/core/simulation.ts:200–221`): the **blue** player's row *r* becomes combat row `r − 1` (rows 1–3 → combat rows 0–2) and the **red** player's row *r* becomes `5 − (r − 1)` (rows 1–3 → combat rows 5–3); columns are unchanged. The combat board has 6 rows (`board.ts:2`), so each team's row 3 sits next to the enemy's row 3 (combat rows 2 and 3) — **row 3 is the front, row 1 the back**.
- *Placement vs movement:* after the fight starts, `MovingState` attacks if an enemy is within range (Chebyshev distance), otherwise walks toward the nearest visible enemy (`moving-state.ts:20–63`, `app/utils/distance.ts:1–4`); some units jump (Dark melee, `moving-state.ts:83–98`). So the board row is a starting position, not a lock. Targeting/pathing details beyond this were not traced.
- *Vespiquen's row-dependent passive* (production reference): when the player moves a Vespiquen to row **1** (back) it gets **range 3 + ATTACK_ORDER**; row **2** → **range 2 + HEAL_ORDER**; row **3** (front) → **range 1 + DEFEND_ORDER** (`app/core/effects/passives.ts:1751–1764`, called from `onPokemonChangePosition`, `game-commands.ts:2449–2512`). The fight entity copies skill and range at fight start (`pokemon-entity.ts:187–194`), so moving during a fight does not change the mode. What each mode does: [pilot-abilities.md](pilot-abilities.md). *(This resolves the earlier "which row is front" question there.)*
- **Unresolved:** a Vespiquen that was never moved to rows 1–3 (e.g. made by evolution) keeps `VESPIQUEN_ORDERS`; the paths that place units without calling `onPokemonChangePosition` were not traced.

## E. Synergies
**In short.** A synergy counts **deployed units only** (not the bench), **once per evolution family**, and a tier switches on when the count reaches that synergy's threshold. Types of units in the same family are merged before counting.

- *Traced:* `computeSynergies` (`app/models/colyseus-models/synergies.ts:97–131`) starts from bonus synergies, then for each unit with `positionY != 0` (bench = 0 excluded) adds the unit's types (including types granted by held items, `:115`, `:287–297`) to a set keyed by evolution family (`PkmFamily`), and counts each type once per family. Thresholds are declared per synergy (`app/config/game/synergies.ts:179–211`, e.g. Dragon 3/5/7, Fire 2/4/6/8, Light 2/3/4/5); a synergy is active at its first threshold (`synergies.ts:22–24`) and `Effects.update` switches on the **highest tier reached** (`app/models/effects.ts:14–25`).
- *Executed* (real `computeSynergies`): Charmander on the bench → no counts; on the board → Dragon 1, Fire 1. Charmander + Charmeleon + Charizard (one family) → Dragon 1, Fire 1, Flying 1. Dragon with 2 families (Charmander, Bagon) → Dragon 2 and no doubling; with 3 families (Charmander, Bagon, Deino) → Dragon 3 and **each dragon's second type also gets +1** (Fire 2, Monster 2, Dark 2).
- **Exceptions (not audited):** *Dragon doubling* (`synergies.ts:133–162`, active once Dragon reaches its first threshold); *FAMILY_OUTING* replaces the family key by a per-unit key, so the same family is not deduplicated (executed: the three Charmander-line units give Dragon 3 and Fire 6 under that rule, the extra Fire coming from Dragon doubling) (`:118–120`, `:141–144`); *dynamic types* for Arceus/Kecleon-type units (`PROTEAN2/3`, `:164–`); item-granted types; bonus synergies from regions/gems; units that cannot be deployed beyond the team-size limit (`app/utils/board.ts:72–78`, enforcement points not traced). Treat any count as "ordinary case only".

## F. Items
**In short.** A unit holds at most **3** items. A **basic component** can be combined with **one other component** the unit already holds into a completed item (if a recipe exists); you can only add a fourth item if it combines. Equipping is checked per item and per unit; **the equip command itself has no phase check.**

- *Declared/Traced* (`OnDragDropItemCommand`, `game-commands.ts:690–965`): the item must be in the player's inventory (`:717`); item-specific `OnItemDroppedEffect`s can veto the drop (`:799–822`, e.g. Eviolite and Rare Candy need an evolution, `core/effects/items.ts:1260–1265`, `:1373–1383`); dishes need an eater (`:824–849`); **unholdable non-consumable** items do nothing when dropped on a unit (`:851–855`), whereas unholdable **consumables** and dishes (TMs, dishes, mulches, flavors, sweets, berries, tickets…; `UnholdableItems` / `ConsumableItems`, `types/enum/Item.ts:1117–1180`) can have effects and may even be used on units that cannot hold items (`:857–863`); a unit with `canHoldItems = false` is refused (`:857–863`); a **full unit (3 items) is refused unless a component combines** (`:871–882`); the same non-component item twice is refused (`:884–892`); with a basic component already held, a second basic one combines through `ItemRecipe` (`types/enum/Item.ts:500–506`) or is refused when there is no recipe (`:894–933`); completed synergy stones are refused on a unit that already has that type (`:935–942`). Moving a unit to the bench returns its removable items (tools, scarves, chef hat, trash; `:2472–2481`, `types/enum/Item.ts:1173–`).
- *Phase restrictions:* the item-drop message handler only requires that the game is not finished (`app/rooms/game-room.ts:420–432`) and the command contains no phase check (none of the lines `:690–965` mention the phase). Selling a deployed unit during a fight **is** blocked (`:981–983`), which is a different command. Whether an item equipped mid-fight affects the running fight (the fight uses copied entities) was not traced.
- **Unresolved:** per-item rules beyond these two examples; recipes and the 100+ item effects.

## G. Two bounded item examples
### EVIOLITE — blocks evolution
- *Traced:* it can be equipped only on a unit with an evolution (`items.ts:1260–1265`). While held it stops that unit from evolving through every rule type: count rule (the holder is not counted as a copy and is not consumed, `count-evolution-handler.ts:35`, `:54`, `:72`), stack (`stack-evolution-handler.ts:8`), item (`item-evolution-handler.ts:17`), state, money, placement and hatch rules (`*-evolution-handler.ts` first line of `canEvolve`). *Declared:* it also gives +100 HP, +10 ATK, +50 AP, +10 DEF, +10 SPE_DEF (`app/config/game/items.ts:79–85`).
- *Cosmog consequence (traced):* a Cosmog holding Eviolite still receives the +10 HP and +1 stack ticks from other units' evolutions (`evolution-manager.ts:92–103`) but `StackEvolutionHandler.canEvolve` returns false, so it does not evolve.

### SHINY_STONE — Light type and spotlight; does it help COSMOEM?
- *Traced — what it does:* it grants its holder the **Light type** (`SynergyGivenByItem`, `types/enum/Item.ts:836`; counted in `synergies.ts:287–297`), +50 AP (`config/game/items.ts:91`), and makes the holder count as in the spotlight in two *different* places: (1) `PokemonEntity.inSpotlight` (`pokemon-entity.ts:302–312`) is true for a unit on the light cell **or** holding the stone (or a Conversion Light unit) and does not itself test whether the Light synergy is active; (2) the passive spotlight callbacks — `Player.onLightChange` (`player.ts:902–916`) and `onPokemonChangePosition` (`game-commands.ts:2487–2497`) — compute `inSpotlight = Light synergy active && (on the light cell || holds the stone)`, so they need an **active Light synergy as well**. For the PRISM/BLOSSOM passives it also triggers a transformation on drop (`items.ts:1457–1468`). The stone-specific "already has this type" refusal (`game-commands.ts:935–942`) covers the nine `SynergyStones` only, and SHINY_STONE is not in that list; I found no rule blocking it on a Light-type unit.
- *Cosmoem's evolution callback* tests exactly three things: `positionX === player.lightX`, `positionY === player.lightY`, and `SynergyTiers[Light].some(e => player.effects.has(e))` (`app/models/colyseus-models/pokemon.ts:14870–14878`). It reads neither `PokemonEntity.inSpotlight` nor the passive spotlight callbacks nor items.
- **Answer:** a Shiny Stone **on the Cosmoem does not make it count as standing on the light cell** — the spotlight notions above and the callback's coordinate test are different things. The stone can matter only *indirectly*: a deployed **holder** adds a Light type to the count, which can switch on the Light tier the callback requires (the Cosmoem already counts as one Light family itself; the stone adds nothing for the same family).
- *Executed* (real `computeSynergies`, `Effects.update`, and Cosmoem's callback; light cell (3,2)): Cosmoem alone → Light 1; Cosmoem + a plain Charmander → Light 1; Cosmoem + a Charmander holding the stone → Light 2 → tier `SHINING_RAY`. Cosmoem **on** (3,2) with no Light tier → **LUNALA**; on (3,2) with the stone-holding Charmander's Light tier → **SOLGALEO**; **off** (3,2) holding the stone itself (item set directly in the probe) with the Light tier → **LUNALA**.
- **Unresolved:** where the light cell is shown to the player and how bonus Light sources interact (see [evolution-context.md](evolution-context.md)).

## H. Ordinary casting and damage foundation (basic attack vs ability)
Evidence types: **Traced** = source read at the pinned revision; **Arithmetic** = calculated from declared values, *not* a combat simulation; **Executed** = see §A/§B probes (nothing new was executed for this section). Scope: the ordinary path; important exceptions are listed, not audited.

**1. Basic attack vs ability damage — different scaling.**
- *Basic attack* (`pokemon-state.ts:attack`): starts from ATK (`:36`), is physical unless SPECIAL_ATTACKS (`:41–43`). **AP does not scale it**, except when it is turned into special (SPECIAL_ATTACKS or Wonder Room): then `ceil(ATK × (1 + AP/100))` (`:85–91`). Crit is rolled on **every** basic attack from `critChance/100` (`:59`; defaults 10 % chance, ×2 power, `battle.ts:8–9`) — no ability-crit flag needed. Order: true-damage split (`:208–212`) → crit factor `1 + (critPower − 1) × reduction` (`:214–217`) → **`Math.round`** of each part (`:234–238`) → `handleDamage` per type (`:239–282`). Extra special parts (Nullify PP, Charge, Teleport…) go through `handleSpecialDamage(…, crit=false, apBoost=false)` (`:257–268`).
- *Ability damage* (typical): the strategy calls `target.handleSpecialDamage(raw, board, AttackType.SPECIAL, caster, crit)` with the default **`apBoost = true`** (`pokemon-entity.ts:346–353`). Order: blocked by protect/skydiving/magic-bounce (`:354–358`) → `raw × (1 + AP/100)` (`:399–400`) → DOUBLE_DAMAGE ×2 (`:401–404`) → strange-steam ×1.2 (`:405–411`) → if `crit`: `× (1 + (critPower − 1) × reduction)`, reduction 0 when the target holds Rocky Helmet **and the damage type is not true** — `handleSpecialDamage` excludes true damage from that negation (`:414–416`); the basic-attack path checks Rocky Helmet separately, without a type test (`pokemon-state.ts:67–70`), −0.1 per Black Augurite (`:412–433`) → `state.handleDamage` (`:435–442`). **No rounding before the defense step.**
- *AP opt-out:* AP applies only when the caller leaves `apBoost` true; callers pass `false` explicitly (e.g. the basic-attack special parts above; Crunch's heal passes AP 0). So "does AP scale ability X?" depends on that ability's call, not on the stat.

**2. What happens inside `handleDamage` (shared by both).** Attacker-side multipliers first (enraged ×2, electric/psychic field ×1.2, … `pokemon-state.ts:455–507`) → type defense division (physical DEF, special SPE_DEF, true none; `:559–566`) → for non-true types flat reductions (`:568–595`) → **`reducedDamage = max(1, ceil())` for every type** (`:597`) → shield first, remainder to HP (`:618–649`, `:726`) → damage-based PP to the target (`:730–732`). Rounding therefore differs: basic attacks are rounded before this function, ability damage is not; both are ceiled after the defense division.

**3. Ability crits.** `castAbility` (`abilities/cast.ts:16–26`) rolls `chance(critChance/100)` **only** if the caster has the `ABILITY_CRIT` effect **or** the strategy sets `canCritByDefault` (default `false`, `ability-strategy.ts:8`); otherwise `crit = false`. The roll is one per cast; the strategy passes it into each `handleSpecialDamage`. Sources of ABILITY_CRIT seen: Reaper Cloth (`effects/items.ts:946–957`), Leek / Large Leek dishes (`effects/dishes.ts:128–145`); others not catalogued. Blast Burn does **not** set `canCritByDefault` (`blast-burn.ts:6–28` extends the base class), so it **cannot crit unless ABILITY_CRIT is present**.

**4. Casting: eligibility, attack slot, PP after.** In `AttackingState.update` the cooldown check comes first (`attacking-state.ts:22`), then **`resetCooldown(1000, speed)` runs unconditionally** (`:24`), then a target is chosen, then: cast if `pp ≥ maxPP && canCast`, else basic attack (`:92–94`). So **a cast uses that attack slot** — no basic attack in the same slot — and the next action is one attack interval later (§C). Casting requires a target in range unless `requiresTarget = false` (`moving-state.ts:40–45`). On cast: `pp = max(0, pp − maxPP)` and `count.ult += 1` (`ability-strategy.ts:15–17`), so PP above `maxPP` carries over; `addPP` clamps at `2×maxPP − 1` (`pokemon-entity.ts:530`). PP at fight start is 0 (`pokemon-entity.ts:97`) before item bonuses. PP gain per basic attack is 5 and per hit taken `ceil(residual/10)` (§B).

**5. Worked example — raw Blast Burn, arithmetic only.** Charmander (1★, raw 30, `blast-burn.ts:14`), target SPE_DEF 3, no crit and none of the other modifiers: AP 0 → 30 ÷ 1.15 = 26.09 → ceil 27. AP 50 → 30 × 1.5 = 45 → 45 ÷ 1.15 = 39.13 → ceil 40. Each +10 AP adds 10 % of the raw 30 (+3) *before* defense. This is the formula applied by hand, **not** a simulated fight (targeting, timing, other modifiers, shields are not modelled).

**Exceptions not audited:** statuses (silence, fatigue, paralysis, wound, armor reduction…), per-ability opt-outs and special call patterns, Power Lens reflection, magic bounce, protect, Shiny Charm/Fossil/resurrection hooks, abilities that move/transform/cast without a target, and any item/synergy that changes AP, crit, speed or PP in combat.

## I. How declared item bonuses become combat stats
- *Declared:* `ItemStats[item]` gives per-stat amounts (data: [item-recipes-stats.json](../../data/07367c34/item-recipes-stats.json)).
- *Traced — initial item path:* the fight entity's constructor copies the board unit's held items into the entity (`pokemon-entity.ts:163–165`) and takes the unit's current stats (`:183–198`, so acquired AP/HP/ATK carry in). `Simulation.addPokemon` then applies synergy effects and **afterwards** `applyItemsEffects` (`simulation.ts:350–351`, `:498–534`), which — after the Pickup/Wonder Box handling — calls `applyItemEffect(item)` directly for each held item (`pokemon-entity.ts:825–846`): first the declared stats through `applyStat`, then the item's effects. `addItem` (`pokemon-entity.ts:782–815`) is a **separate acquisition path** for adding an item to an entity (for example `uxie.addItem(Item.AQUA_EGG)` in `abilities/hidden-power.ts:368`); it also adds the item to the set before calling `applyItemEffect`. `applyStat` maps (`:1399–1437`): ATK → `addAttack`, DEF → `addDefense`, SPE_DEF → `addSpecialDefense`, **AP → `addAbilityPower`** (flat, added to current AP; Nullify Bandanna converts it, see [silk-scarf-items.md](silk-scarf-items.md)), **PP → `addPP`** (adds to *current* PP, subject to the `addPP` rules in §B; **it does not change `maxPP`**), SPEED, CRIT_CHANCE, CRIT_POWER, SHIELD → `addShield`, HP → `addMaxHP` (also raises current HP), LUCK, RANGE (direct). `Stat.MAX_PP` exists as an enum value but `applyStat` has no case for it and no `ItemStats` entry uses it, so **declared item bonuses never raise `maxPP`** in this path.
- *Starting PP, ordinary case:* PP is 0 when the entity is created (`pokemon-entity.ts:97`); the held items' PP bonuses are then added through `addPP` when `applyItemsEffects` runs (clamped to `2×maxPP − 1`; e.g. Efficient Bandanna's 15, Mystic Water's 15). This is only the ordinary case: other start hooks (`OnSimulationStartEffect`/`OnSpawnEffect`), dishes, passives and synergy effects can also change PP at fight start and were not audited.
- *maxPP changes seen:* Efficient Bandanna (`effects/items.ts:1536–1548`, multiplies maxPP by 0.85, rounded, for units on its cell and the cells left/right, at fight start, `simulation.ts:239–262`); reset/transform paths (`items.ts:551`, `evolution-handler.ts:91`, `abilities/transform.ts:28`). Not catalogued beyond these.
- *Arithmetic example (not a simulation):* a Charmander (maxPP 100) holding Efficient Bandanna: maxPP `round(0.85 × 100) = 85`, starting PP 15, so 70 more PP are needed for the first cast: 14 basic attacks at +5 each (20 without the item), before counting PP from damage taken. If another unit holds the Bandanna and the Charmander stands on the same row within one column, only the 0.85 applies (no PP bonus): 85 PP needed. After the first cast PP drops by 85, not 100. Two overlapping Bandannas multiply (each rounds).

## What is usable, what is not
Usable as the player-facing explanation of the *rules read here*: the basic-attack vs ability damage order, ability crits and the cast slot (§H), how declared item bonuses become combat stats (§I), the damage defense step, PP gain/cast rule, the speed→cooldown conversion, row geometry and the Vespiquen modes, ordinary synergy counting, item slots/combination, and the two item examples. **Not** usable as complete rules: the full damage pipeline, PP exceptions, speed modifiers, synergy exceptions, per-item behavior, or anything about the live game. Questions that fall into the unresolved items above should be answered with that uncertainty.
