## A. Damage and defenses
**In short.** Every hit has a *type*. **Physical** damage is reduced by the target's **DEF**, **special** damage by its **SPE_DEF**, and **true** damage ignores both. The normal reduction is `damage ÷ (1 + 0.05 × defense)`, rounded **up**, and at least 1 for non-true hits. Shields absorb damage before HP.

- *Declared:* `ARMOR_FACTOR = 0.05` (`app/config/game/battle.ts:5`).
- *Traced — the reduction step* (`app/core/pokemon-state.ts:handleDamage`, `:559–566`): physical → `damage / (1 + ARMOR_FACTOR × def)`; special → `… × speDef`; true → unchanged. Then, for non-true types only, some flat reductions apply (Poké Doll ×0.7, Guts/Sturdy/Defiant/Coaching −3/−6/−9/−12, Wonder Guard −20; `:568–595`), then `reducedDamage = max(1, ceil(reducedDamage))` (`:597`). Shields take the damage first, the remainder hits HP (`:618–649`, `:726`). A target with *armor reduction* uses half its defenses (`:509–514`).
- *Traced — where the "incoming damage" comes from.* A basic attack starts from the attacker's ATK (`pokemon-state.ts:attack`, `:36`), is physical unless the attacker has the SPECIAL_ATTACKS effect (`:41–43`), is multiplied on a crit by `1 + (critPower − 1) × reduction` (`:214–217`; default crit chance 10 %, power 2 — `app/config/game/battle.ts:8–9`) and rounded (`:235`); ability damage is dealt by the ability code, usually through `handleSpecialDamage`, which first adds the attacker's AP (`damage × (1 + AP/100)`, `app/core/pokemon-entity.ts:399–400`, default `apBoost = true`).
- *Arithmetic example (assumptions: no crit, no AP, no shield/status/item/weather/field modifiers, target hp ≥ the damage):* a physical hit of **20** on a target with **DEF 10**: `20 ÷ (1 + 0.05 × 10) = 13.33…` → ceil → **14** HP. The same 20 as special on SPE_DEF 10 is also 14; as true damage it is 20.
- *Executed* (real `handleDamage`, stub target, no modifiers): 30 physical vs DEF 10 → 20; 30 special vs SPE_DEF 20 → 15; 30 true → 30; 1 physical vs DEF 100 → 1 (the floor); 30 vs DEF 0 → 30; 30 physical vs DEF 10 with a 10 shield → shield 10 → 0 and HP −10 (reduced hit 20).
- **Not universal / unresolved:** many attacker- and target-side multipliers sit *before* the defense step (enrage ×2, Electric/Psychic/Grass/Fairy fields ×1.2, Misty/Bloodmoon weather ×1.2, Sheer Cold ×1.3, …; `pokemon-state.ts:455–507`), and others around it (magic bounce, protect, dodge, Power Lens, Shiny Charm, Fossil, resurrection; death rules `:650–800`). They were **not** traced one by one; the formula above is the *defense step only*.

## B. PP (mana) and casting
**In short.** Each unit has a PP bar with maximum `maxPP`. A unit **casts instead of basic-attacking** when its PP reaches `maxPP`. PP comes mainly from **attacking** (+5 per basic attack) and from **taking damage** (+⌈HP lost ÷ 10⌉). After a cast the bar is reduced by `maxPP`, so extra PP carries over.

- *Declared:* `ON_ATTACK_MANA = 5` (`battle.ts:3`).
- *Traced — gain:* `onAttack` adds 5 (`pokemon-entity.ts:911–932`); `handleDamage` adds `ceil(residualDamage / 10)` to the target when the hit is flagged to give mana (`pokemon-state.ts:730–732`; residual = damage that reached HP, after the shield). `addPP` clamps the bar to `0 … 2×maxPP − 1`, blocks positive gains while silenced, protected, resurrecting or under NO_PP_GAIN, and halves positive gains under fatigue (`pokemon-entity.ts:508–532`). PP starts at 0 in a fight (field default `pokemon-entity.ts:97`).
- *Traced — trigger:* each time the attack cooldown ends, the unit casts if `pp >= maxPP && canCast` (`app/core/attacking-state.ts:92–94`), otherwise it basic-attacks; the cast uses that attack slot. `canCast` is false when silenced, holding Nullify Bandana, or with a pending Teleport (`pokemon-entity.ts:250–256`). Abilities flagged `requiresTarget = false` may cast without a target (`app/core/moving-state.ts:40–45`).
- *Traced — after casting:* `AbilityStrategy.process` sets `pp = max(0, pp − maxPP)` and counts the cast (`app/core/abilities/ability-strategy.ts:16–17`); `castAbility` can roll a crit only with ABILITY_CRIT (or a `canCritByDefault` ability) (`app/core/abilities/cast.ts:18–26`).
- **Exceptions seen, not catalogued:** items/effects that add or remove PP (Shiny Charm +50 PP, Nullify Bandana, Twist Band, …), abilities that manipulate PP themselves, and per-ability rules (see [pilot-abilities.md](pilot-abilities.md) for three examples).

## C. Attack speed
**In short.** The "speed" stat is **not** attacks per second. The wait between a unit's attack decisions is `round(1000 / (0.4 + 0.007 × speed))` in the simulation's time units (milliseconds, see caveat), so higher speed shortens the wait with diminishing returns.

- *Traced:* `AttackingState.update` resets the cooldown with `resetCooldown(1000, speed)` (`attacking-state.ts:22–24`; paralysis halves the speed used); `PokemonEntity.resetCooldown` computes `round(baseDuration / (0.4 + speed × 0.007))` (`pokemon-entity.ts:319–321`). `getAttackTimings` uses the same factor for the animation (`attacking-state.ts:126–146`).
- *Executed* (real `resetCooldown` / `getAttackTimings`): speed 0 → 2500; 50 (the default, `battle.ts:7`) → 1333; 100 → 909; 200 → 556; 300 → 400. *Arithmetic:* the reciprocal gives ≈ 0.40, 0.75, 1.10, 1.80 and 2.50 attacks per second *if* one cooldown unit is one millisecond.
- *Caveat (inferred):* the cooldown is decreased by the simulation delta passed from the room (`game-commands.ts:1178`, from Colyseus' simulation interval); that this delta is in milliseconds was **not** traced into the library, but the `1000`/`500` constants and comments assume it.
- Movement is separate: one cell costs `round(500 / (0.5 + speed/100))` units (`moving-state.ts:23`, `app/core/move-speed.ts:3–9`; executed factor 1 at speed 50). Buffs/debuffs on speed and how range and travel time affect when damage lands (`attacking-state.ts:131–144`) were not traced further.

## D. Positioning
**In short.** On the player board, **row 0 is the bench** and **rows 1–3** are the battlefield; **row 1 is the back row and row 3 the front row** for both teams. Placement only decides where a unit *starts*; during combat units walk toward enemies.

- *Declared/Traced:* units can only be dropped on `y < BOARD_SIDE_HEIGHT` (= 4, "0 = bench"; `app/config/game/board.ts:3`, `game-commands.ts:335–343`). At fight start the simulation copies only non-bench units (`app/core/simulation.ts:200–221`): the **blue** player's row *r* becomes combat row `r − 1` (rows 1–3 → combat rows 0–2) and the **red** player's row *r* becomes `5 − (r − 1)` (rows 1–3 → combat rows 5–3); columns are unchanged. The combat board has 6 rows (`board.ts:2`), so each team's row 3 sits next to the enemy's row 3 (combat rows 2 and 3) — **row 3 is the front, row 1 the back**.
- *Placement vs movement:* after the fight starts, `MovingState` attacks if an enemy is within range (Chebyshev distance), otherwise walks toward the nearest visible enemy (`moving-state.ts:20–63`, `app/utils/distance.ts:1–4`); some units jump (Dark melee, `moving-state.ts:83–98`). So the board row is a starting position, not a lock. Targeting/pathing details beyond this were not traced.
- *Vespiquen's row-dependent passive* (production reference): when the player moves a Vespiquen to row **1** (back) it gets **range 3 + ATTACK_ORDER**; row **2** → **range 2 + HEAL_ORDER**; row **3** (front) → **range 1 + DEFEND_ORDER** (`app/core/effects/passives.ts:1751–1764`, called from `onPokemonChangePosition`, `game-commands.ts:2449–2512`). The fight entity copies skill and range at fight start (`pokemon-entity.ts:187–194`), so moving during a fight does not change the mode. What each mode does: [pilot-abilities.md](pilot-abilities.md). *(This resolves the earlier "which row is front" question there.)*
- **Unresolved:** a Vespiquen that was never moved to rows 1–3 (e.g. made by evolution) keeps `VESPIQUEN_ORDERS`; the paths that place units without calling `onPokemonChangePosition` were not traced.

## E. Synergies
**In short.** A synergy counts **deployed units only** (not the bench), **once per evolution family**, and a tier switches on when the count reaches that synergy's threshold. Types of units in the same family are merged before counting.

- *Traced:* `computeSynergies` (`app/models/colyseus-models/synergies.ts:97–131`) starts from bonus synergies, then for each unit with `positionY != 0` (bench = 0 excluded) adds the unit's types (including types granted by held items, `:115`, `:287–297`) to a set keyed by evolution family (`PkmFamily`), and counts each type once per family. Thresholds are declared per synergy (`app/config/game/synergies.ts:179–211`, e.g. Dragon 3/5/7, Fire 2/4/6/8, Light 2/3/4/5); a synergy is active at its first threshold (`synergies.ts:22–24`) and `Effects.update` switches on the **highest tier reached** (`app/models/effects.ts:14–25`).
- *Executed* (real `computeSynergies`): Charmander on the bench → no counts; on the board → Dragon 1, Fire 1. Charmander + Charmeleon + Charizard (one family) → Dragon 1, Fire 1, Flying 1. Dragon with 2 families (Charmander, Bagon) → Dragon 2 and no doubling; with 3 families (Charmander, Bagon, Deino) → Dragon 3 and **each dragon's second type also gets +1** (Fire 2, Monster 2, Dark 2).
- **Exceptions (not audited):** *Dragon doubling* (`synergies.ts:133–162`, active once Dragon reaches its first threshold); *FAMILY_OUTING* replaces the family key by a per-unit key, so the same family is not deduplicated (executed: the three Charmander-line units give Dragon 3 and Fire 6 under that rule, the extra Fire coming from Dragon doubling) (`:118–120`, `:141–144`); *dynamic types* for Arceus/Kecleon-type units (`PROTEAN2/3`, `:164–`); item-granted types; bonus synergies from regions/gems; units that cannot be deployed beyond the team-size limit (`app/utils/board.ts:72–78`, enforcement points not traced). Treat any count as "ordinary case only".

## F. Items
**In short.** A unit holds at most **3** items. A **basic component** can be combined with **one other component** the unit already holds into a completed item (if a recipe exists); you can only add a fourth item if it combines. Equipping is checked per item and per unit; **the equip command itself has no phase check.**

- *Declared/Traced* (`OnDragDropItemCommand`, `game-commands.ts:690–965`): the item must be in the player's inventory (`:717`); item-specific `OnItemDroppedEffect`s can veto the drop (`:799–822`, e.g. Eviolite and Rare Candy need an evolution, `core/effects/items.ts:1260–1265`, `:1373–1383`); dishes need an eater (`:824–849`); non-holdable items do nothing; a unit with `canHoldItems = false` is refused (`:857–863`); a **full unit (3 items) is refused unless a component combines** (`:871–882`); the same non-component item twice is refused (`:884–892`); with a basic component already held, a second basic one combines through `ItemRecipe` (`types/enum/Item.ts:500–506`) or is refused when there is no recipe (`:894–933`); completed synergy stones are refused on a unit that already has that type (`:935–942`). Moving a unit to the bench returns its removable items (tools, scarves, chef hat, trash; `:2472–2481`, `types/enum/Item.ts:1173–`).
- *Phase restrictions:* the item-drop message handler only requires that the game is not finished (`app/rooms/game-room.ts:420–432`) and the command contains no phase check (none of the lines `:690–965` mention the phase). Selling a deployed unit during a fight **is** blocked (`:981–983`), which is a different command. Whether an item equipped mid-fight affects the running fight (the fight uses copied entities) was not traced.
- **Unresolved:** per-item rules beyond these two examples; recipes and the 100+ item effects.

## G. Two bounded item examples
### EVIOLITE — blocks evolution
- *Traced:* it can be equipped only on a unit with an evolution (`items.ts:1260–1265`). While held it stops that unit from evolving through every rule type: count rule (the holder is not counted as a copy and is not consumed, `count-evolution-handler.ts:35`, `:54`, `:72`), stack (`stack-evolution-handler.ts:8`), item (`item-evolution-handler.ts:17`), state, money, placement and hatch rules (`*-evolution-handler.ts` first line of `canEvolve`). *Declared:* it also gives +100 HP, +10 ATK, +50 AP, +10 DEF, +10 SPE_DEF (`app/config/game/items.ts:79–85`).
- *Cosmog consequence (traced):* a Cosmog holding Eviolite still receives the +10 HP and +1 stack ticks from other units' evolutions (`evolution-manager.ts:92–103`) but `StackEvolutionHandler.canEvolve` returns false, so it does not evolve.

### SHINY_STONE — Light type and spotlight; does it help COSMOEM?
- *Traced — what it does:* it grants its holder the **Light type** (`SynergyGivenByItem`, `types/enum/Item.ts:836`; counted in `synergies.ts:287–297`), +50 AP (`config/game/items.ts:91`), and counts the holder as **in the spotlight** for passives and fights regardless of position (`app/models/colyseus-models/player.ts:902–916`, `pokemon-entity.ts:302–312`, `game-commands.ts:2490–2497`). For the PRISM/BLOSSOM passives it also triggers a transformation on drop (`items.ts:1457–1468`). The stone-specific "already has this type" refusal (`game-commands.ts:935–942`) covers the nine `SynergyStones` only, and SHINY_STONE is not in that list; I found no rule blocking it on a Light-type unit.
- *Cosmoem's evolution callback* tests exactly three things: `positionX === player.lightX`, `positionY === player.lightY`, and `SynergyTiers[Light].some(e => player.effects.has(e))` (`app/models/colyseus-models/pokemon.ts:14870–14878`). It does **not** read `inSpotlight` or items.
- **Answer:** a Shiny Stone **on the Cosmoem does not make it count as standing on the light cell** — the spotlight flag and the callback's coordinate test are different things. The stone can matter only *indirectly*: a deployed **holder** adds a Light type to the count, which can switch on the Light tier the callback requires (the Cosmoem already counts as one Light family itself; the stone adds nothing for the same family).
- *Executed* (real `computeSynergies`, `Effects.update`, and Cosmoem's callback; light cell (3,2)): Cosmoem alone → Light 1; Cosmoem + a plain Charmander → Light 1; Cosmoem + a Charmander holding the stone → Light 2 → tier `SHINING_RAY`. Cosmoem **on** (3,2) with no Light tier → **LUNALA**; on (3,2) with the stone-holding Charmander's Light tier → **SOLGALEO**; **off** (3,2) holding the stone itself (item set directly in the probe) with the Light tier → **LUNALA**.
- **Unresolved:** where the light cell is shown to the player and how bonus Light sources interact (see [evolution-context.md](evolution-context.md)).

## What is usable, what is not
Usable as the player-facing explanation of the *rules read here*: the damage defense step, PP gain/cast rule, the speed→cooldown conversion, row geometry and the Vespiquen modes, ordinary synergy counting, item slots/combination, and the two item examples. **Not** usable as complete rules: the full damage pipeline, PP exceptions, speed modifiers, synergy exceptions, per-item behavior, or anything about the live game. Questions that fall into the unresolved items above should be answered with that uncertainty.
